Kadiliman Esports Stadium

659-A, Brgy, Manila Real Residences, 1129 Natividad Lopez St, Ermita, Manila, 1000 Metro Manila

It is situated along the main university and commercial belt around Concepcion Street / San Marcelino Street, just steps away from the main entrances of SM City Manila, Manila City Hall, and nearby schools such as Adamson University and Technological University of the Philippines (TUP).

Ryzon Internet Cafe

806 San Marcelino St. Brgy 664 Ermita Manila

Ryzon Internet Cafe is a popular gaming spot located along san marcelino street in front of Gate 4 exit of TUP Manila. It offers a variety of gaming PCs and consoles for gamers to enjoy their favorite games with friends.

KROMA Esports Cafe & Billiards

San marcelino, 1000

KROMA Esports Cafe & Billiards is a vibrant gaming and social hub located in the along san marcelino street in Ermita,just beside ryzon dimsum treats in front of Gate 4 exit of TUP Manila. It provides a comfortable environment for gamers to play their favorite titles while enjoying the company of friends.
